4. Markov Decision Process Demo

Back to Contents
This simple applet models investment portfolio management as a Markov Decision Process. In this problem, at the beginning of each trading period (1 year), the manager rebalances the portfolio based on their knowledge of the previous year's market condition (bear, normal, bull), adopting one of the following strategies: all stocks, aggressive, moderate, conservative, all cash. A risk-adjusted return function, \(E[\text{return}] - \lambda \sigma^2 [\text{return}]\), where \(\lambda\) is the risk-aversion level, is used to compute the Bellman equation. You can compare the performance of the optimal strategy against benchmark portfolios.
The transition probabilities between the market conditions, the weights of each asset under different strategies, and the returns of the assets are given in the tables.
