I passed the JLPT N1 exam with full marks after learning Japanese on my own for 2.5 years. I am a fan of Japanese mystery novels and Japanese literature in general. I take great joy in being able to read novels in their original language. My next goal is to build more confidence in speaking (the JLPT is flawed as it only tests passive skills).

Below is a list of novels that I have finished reading in Japanese. This is not a list of my "favorites". The purpose of this list is to keep myself motivated and to help others find books at the right level. I marked the approximate levels (N5 easiest, N1 hardest) for each one based on my personal reading experience and to the best of my recollection, though these estimates may not be accurate, as prior knowledge of Chinese or Korean provides significant advantage.

Realistically, one needs at least N3 level of grammar and vocabulary to be able to read novels at all. At N2 level you should be able to read most casual contemporary fictions such as mystery and thrillers comfortably. From my experience, building a large vocabulary is much more important than memorizing esoteric grammars. A vocabulary much larger than N1 level is needed to minimize interruptions when reading literary novels. I recommend this book and this book for improving vocabulary.

I look forward to adding more to the list.
